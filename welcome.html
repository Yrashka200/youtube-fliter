YouTube Feed Filter

Quick setup guide

👋
Welcome!
Thanks for installing YouTube Feed Filter.

This extension helps you clean up your YouTube feed — hide unwanted categories, block ads, filter by language and much more.

Let's take a quick tour of what it can do.
🚫
Hide by category
Block entire topics from your feed with one click:
🎮
Games — gameplay, streams, let's plays
⚽
Sports — football, UFC, highlights
🏛
Politics — news, elections, debates
🎵
Music — songs, remixes, clips
💻
Tech, Crypto, Kids, News, Movies…
The extension detects videos by title, channel and description.
🌍
Filter by language
Don't understand a language? Hide it entirely.
🇷🇺
Hide Russian — removes Cyrillic videos from your feed
🇬🇧
Hide English — removes English videos
Language is auto-detected from the title and channel name using Cyrillic/Latin ratio and common word markers.
🔤
Custom block words
Have a specific topic you never want to see?
Add your own keywords and any video containing them will be hidden.
✏️
Example: standup, reaction, drama
⚡
Applied instantly — no page reload needed
🧩
Works together with category filters
📱
Shorts & watched videos
Get rid of the clutter you've already seen.
🚫
Hide Shorts — removes the entire Shorts shelf and individual Shorts from the feed
👁
Hide watched — hides videos you've watched more than 90%
📐
Video size
Adjust how many videos fit in a row — perfect for any screen.
🔹
Small — 6 videos per row
🔸
Medium — 4 videos per row
🔶
Large — 2 videos per row
🎦
Cinema — 1 big video per row, perfect for browsing
🛡
Ad blocking
Built-in ad blocker that removes ads at multiple levels.
📺
Skips video ads automatically (mutes + fast-forwards + clicks Skip)
🧱
Hides banner ads, promoted videos, merch shelves
🚫
Blocks anti-adblock popups ("Ad blockers violate YouTube's Terms…")
🌐
Blocks requests to doubleclick.net, googlesyndication.com and more
⚡
Optimization mode
Makes YouTube run smoothly even on weak devices and long scrolling sessions.
🎞
Disable animations — removes all transitions and CSS animations
📹
Pause preview videos — stops the CPU-hungry hover previews on cards
✨
Disable effects — no shadows, blur, ambient mode around the player
🔍
Reduce scanning — extension scans the page less often
🧠
Virtualize DOM — off-screen cards are removed from rendering, so even 500+ videos scroll at 60 FPS
Each sub-option can be toggled individually in the popup menu.
💾
Profiles & Backup
Save different filter presets for different moods.
👤
Profiles — create "Work", "Kids", "Chill" etc. and switch instantly
⬇
Export — save all settings & profiles to a JSON file
⬆
Import — restore them on any device
🚀
You're all set!
The extension auto-checks for updates every hour from GitHub. You'll see a NEW badge on the toolbar icon when a new version is available.
