Make your feed yours

YouTube Feed Filter works on your YouTube pages. Your choices are saved in a profile you can switch or edit from the extension popup.

You’ll choose topics and words to hide, language filters, Shorts and watched-video behavior, video layout, ads, and performance options. Existing profiles are kept as they are.

Later, the popup also lets you create more profiles, start a timed Focus session, customize the YouTube background, view watch-time stats, and import or export a backup.

Choose topics to hide

Select any categories you don’t want in your recommendations. Leave them all unchecked to keep every topic.

The extension checks video titles, channel names, and descriptions for topic signals. Topic matching is automatic; you can fine-tune it later in the popup.

Filter language and specific words

Use language filters for broad cleanup, or add your own words for specific topics you never want to see.

Language is estimated from video titles and channel names. Custom keywords are matched against video text; they work alongside category filters.

Separate multiple words or phrases with commas.

Shape your viewing experience

Choose what gets removed and how many videos you see across a row.

“Hide watched” removes videos watched past 90%. Picture-in-Picture controls whether YouTube’s PiP button is available. You can adjust any of these later.

Ads and performance

Choose whether to use the extension’s ad controls and optional performance optimizations.

Ad controls can hide page promotions and handle some video ads. YouTube changes frequently, so ad filtering may not catch every ad. Optimization reduces effects and previews; it can change how YouTube looks.

Name your profile

This saves your choices as a new profile and makes it active. Your existing profiles and settings will not be deleted.

Profiles are separate sets of settings: for example, make one for Work and another for relaxing. Switch or edit them any time from the profile row in the extension popup.

You can create additional profiles, focus sessions, themes, and backups from the extension popup.
